Whales now swim into conversations, with separate glass for the background and composer.

- Added **Show whales in chat**, on by default: the ocean keeps swimming behind messages in existing conversations. Turn it off to restore the empty-chat-only behavior.
- Conversations gain a mist layer between whales and messages (translucent base, gradients and backdrop blur). Text, code and controls stay sharp and menus keep their position. Both themes use neutral white/gray with no blue tint.
- Glass is split into **Frosted-glass chat background** and **Frosted-glass composer**, each with its own blur and opacity right below its switch. Defaults: background **19 px / 0.45**, composer **14 px / 0.45**, matching the previous look.
- Existing preferences are preserved: the former shared glass switch, blur and opacity carry over to the background, so nothing changes visually after upgrading.
